We recently underwent a big project in our lab involving lots of work with our dilution refrigerator infrastructure. We purchased a new fridge! Unfortunately in order to fit the GHS for the new fridge into our space, we had to move one of our existing fridges out of the way. I spearheaded the project of disassembling the existing fridge, moving it, installing the new fridge, then reassembling the existing fridge in its new location. We had assistance from Bluefors for the setup of the new fridge, but we did the disassembly and reassembly of the existing fridge on our own. To make things a bit more complicated, the fridge that we moved had a pneumatic vibration damping system involving a second frame nested outside of the usual fridge frame and a passive damping block on a third external frame with the t-dampers. Well, there is no better way to really learn how something works than to take it apart and put it back together!
